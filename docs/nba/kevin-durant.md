# Kevin Durant

Kevin Wayne Durant is a small forward and power forward for the Houston Rockets. He was born on September 29, 1988, in Washington, D.C., and grew up in Prince George's County, Maryland. He is listed at 6-foot-11 and 240 pounds (about 7 feet in shoes). Nicknames include the Slim Reaper and Easy Money Sniper. He is widely regarded as one of the greatest scorers ever. Seattle took him second overall in 2007 after Greg Oden.

## Early life and college

Durant's father left when he was an infant; his mother Wanda and grandmother Barbara Davis raised him until his father returned around age 13. He wore No. 35 for AAU coach Charles Craig, who was murdered at 35. High school stops: National Christian Academy, Oak Hill Academy, and Montrose Christian. He was McDonald's All-American co-MVP in 2006.

He chose Texas over more famous programs to "set my own path." As a freshman he averaged 25.8 points and 11.1 rebounds, became the first freshman named Naismith College Player of the Year, and won the Wooden Award. Texas retired No. 35.

## Seattle / Oklahoma City (2007–2016)

Durant won Rookie of the Year at 20.3 points, joining LeBron James and Carmelo Anthony as teenagers to average 20. The SuperSonics moved to Oklahoma City in 2008 and drafted Russell Westbrook. Durant won scoring titles in 2010 (youngest ever, 30.1), 2011, and 2012. In 2012 the Thunder reached the Finals and lost to LeBron's Miami Heat; Durant averaged 30.6 in the series.

In 2012–13 he joined the 50–40–90 club as the youngest member. In 2013–14 he averaged 32.0 points and won MVP, including a 54-point game and a 41-game streak of 25-plus that passed Michael Jordan. Foot injuries wrecked 2014–15. In 2016 Oklahoma City went up 3–1 on the 73-win Warriors in the West finals, then lost in seven.

## Golden State Warriors (2016–2019)

On July 4, 2016, Durant announced in The Players' Tribune that he would sign with Golden State, a move many fans called taking the easy path after that 3–1 collapse. He won titles and Finals MVP in 2017 and 2018 against LeBron's Cavaliers, averaging 35.2 in the 2017 Finals. The 2017 Warriors went 16–1 in the playoffs. In 2019 he tore his Achilles in Finals Game 5 against Toronto after a calf strain earlier in the postseason. Golden State lost the series. The franchise stopped issuing No. 35.

## Brooklyn, Phoenix, Houston (2019–present)

Durant signed with Brooklyn in 2019 and sat out 2019–20 rehabbing. In 2021 he scored 49 and 48 in Games 5 and 7 of the East semis against Milwaukee; the Game 7 48 was then a Game 7 record, but a toe on the line turned a potential winner into a two, and the Nets lost in overtime. Boston swept Brooklyn in 2022.

He requested a trade, then went to Phoenix in February 2023 with T.J. Warren. In eight Suns regular-season games he went 8–0 and finished the year as the first player to shoot 55–40–90. Denver beat Phoenix in the 2023 West semis. Minnesota swept the Suns in 2024. In February 2025 he reached 30,000 points. Phoenix missed the playoffs that year.

Houston acquired him in July 2025 in a seven-team deal, the largest in NBA history. In 2025–26 he passed Wilt Chamberlain, Dirk Nowitzki, and Michael Jordan on the all-time scoring list to rank fifth. He became the first player with a 40-point game for five franchises and the first with All-NBA honors for five franchises. The Rockets lost to the Lakers in the first round; Durant played only Game 2 because of an ankle sprain.

## Championships and honors

Durant has two NBA titles (2017, 2018), two Finals MVPs, one regular-season MVP (2014), four scoring titles, Rookie of the Year (2008), two All-Star Game MVPs, 16 All-Star selections, and 12 All-NBA teams. He is on the NBA 75th Anniversary Team. He ranks fifth in NBA career scoring.

## Playing style

At nearly seven feet with a 7-foot-4 wingspan, Durant gets a clean look from anywhere. Isolation scorer early, then an elite off-ball and catch-and-shoot weapon with the Warriors. He can finish at the rim and shoot 50–40–90. Defense and passing were early knocks; both improved, and he was a DPOY candidate in Golden State.

## Olympics and Team USA

Durant is the only man with four Olympic basketball golds (2012, 2016, 2020, 2024) and also won the 2010 FIBA World Cup (tournament MVP). He is Team USA's Olympic scoring and rebounding leader (518 points, 137 rebounds) and was Olympics MVP in Tokyo. In Paris 2024 he passed Lisa Leslie for U.S. Olympic basketball scoring, men or women.

## Off the court

He is close with his mother; Lifetime made The Real MVP: The Wanda Durant Story. He wears Nike, founded Thirty Five Ventures with Rich Kleiman, and produced the Oscar-winning short Two Distant Strangers. Seat Pleasant, Maryland, named August 17 Kevin Durant Day.

## Career statistics

Through 2025–26, Durant's regular-season line is 1,201 games, 36.7 minutes, 27.1 points, 6.9 rebounds, 4.4 assists, 1.0 steals, and 1.1 blocks, on .503 / .392 / .882 shooting. Playoffs: 29.3 points in 171 games. College at Texas: 25.8 points and 11.1 rebounds in 35 games.

Season highs include 32.0 points (2014 MVP year) and 6.4 assists (2021–22). Playoff high: 34.3 points per game in 2021.
