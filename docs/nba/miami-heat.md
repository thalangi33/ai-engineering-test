# Miami Heat

The Miami Heat are an NBA team in the Eastern Conference's Southeast Division. They began as a 1988 expansion club (with Orlando, Charlotte, and Minnesota) and play at Kaseya Center. Colors: black, red, yellow. Micky Arison has owned the team since 1995; Pat Riley is team president; Erik Spoelstra is head coach. They have won three NBA championships.

## Championships and records

Three titles: 2006, 2012, 2013. Seven conference titles (2006, 2011–14, 2020, 2023). Sixteen division titles. Retired numbers include 1 (Chris Bosh), 3 (Dwyane Wade), 10 (Tim Hardaway), 23 (Michael Jordan, honorary — he never played for Miami), 32 (Shaquille O'Neal), 33 (Alonzo Mourning), 40 (Udonis Haslem).

The 2012–13 Heat won 27 straight, third-longest in NBA history, and 66 games, a franchise record. LeBron James won two MVPs in Miami. In March 2026 Bam Adebayo scored 83 against Washington, the Heat single-game record, passing James's 61 and second only to Chamberlain's 100.

## Eras

Early years were mediocre. Riley arrived in 1995, traded for Mourning and Hardaway, and built a physical East contender. The 1996–97 team went 61–21 and lost the conference finals to Jordan's Bulls. The Knicks were the chief playoff rival from 1998 to 2000.

Wade, drafted fifth in 2003 out of Marquette, and a trade for O'Neal produced the 2006 title. Riley took the coaching job back midseason from Stan Van Gundy. Dallas won the first two Finals games; Miami won the next four. Wade was Finals MVP. After O'Neal was traded, the Heat bottomed out at 15–67 in 2007–08. Spoelstra replaced Riley on the bench in 2008.

The 2010 Big Three — James, Wade, and Chris Bosh — reached four straight Finals: loss to Dallas in 2011, titles over Oklahoma City (2012) and San Antonio (2013, Ray Allen's Game 6 corner three), loss to the Spurs in 2014. James returned to Cleveland in 2014. Wade left for Chicago in 2016, came back, and retired a Heat lifer. Bosh's career ended because of blood clots.

Jimmy Butler, Bam Adebayo, and Tyler Herro made the 2020 bubble Finals (loss to LeBron's Lakers) and the 2023 Finals as an eighth seed (loss to Denver) after upsetting top-seeded Milwaukee. Butler was traded to Golden State in February 2025. Giannis Antetokounmpo arrived from Milwaukee in July 2026.
