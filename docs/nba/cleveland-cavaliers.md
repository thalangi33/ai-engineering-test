# Cleveland Cavaliers

The Cleveland Cavaliers (Cavs) are an NBA team in the Eastern Conference's Central Division. Founded as a 1970 expansion club with Portland and Buffalo, they play at Rocket Arena in downtown Cleveland (shared with the AHL Monsters). Colors: wine, gold, black. Dan Gilbert has been majority owner since 2005; Usher and Browns star Myles Garrett hold minority stakes.

## Championships and records

One NBA title: 2016. Five conference titles (2007, 2015–18). Eight division titles, including 2025. The 2016 Finals win over Golden State was the first time a team came back from 3–1 in the Finals, and Cleveland's first major pro sports title since the 1964 Browns, ending talk of a city curse.

Retired numbers include 7 (Bingo Smith), 11 (Zydrunas Ilgauskas), 22 (Larry Nance), 25 (Mark Price), 34 (Austin Carr). LeBron James's 23 is not retired; he left twice.

## Eras

The Cavs lost their first 15 games in 1970–71. Bill Fitch's 1976 team (Austin Carr, Bingo Smith, Jim Chones, Nate Thurmond) won the Miracle at Richfield over Washington on Dick Snyder's late shot, then lost the East finals to Boston after Chones broke a toe.

Ted Stepien owned the club in the early 1980s: six coaches, bad trades of future first-rounders, a 24-game losing streak, and $15 million in losses. The NBA created the Stepien Rule so teams cannot trade consecutive first-round picks. George and Gordon Gund bought the team in 1983. Mark Price, Brad Daugherty, Ron Harper, and Larry Nance made the late-80s/90s Cavs regular playoff teams; they reached the 1992 East finals. Jordan's "The Shot" over Craig Ehlo in 1989 is the signature playoff wound of that era.

Cleveland drafted LeBron James first overall in 2003 out of nearby Akron. With Ilgauskas they reached the 2007 Finals and were swept by San Antonio. The 2008–09 team won 66 games, then lost to Orlando in the East finals. James announced The Decision in 2010 and left for Miami. Cleveland went 19–63, had a 26-game losing streak, and won the lottery for Kyrie Irving in 2011.

James returned in 2014. A Big Three with Irving and Kevin Love went to four straight Finals against Golden State and won in 2016. Irving was traded to Boston in 2017; James left for the Lakers in 2018. Donovan Mitchell arrived in 2022. The 2024–25 Cavs started 15–0, tied for the second-best start in NBA history, before Boston ended the streak.
