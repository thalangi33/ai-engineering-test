# Nikola Jokić

Nikola Jokić is a center for the Denver Nuggets. He was born on February 19, 1995, in Sombor, Serbia. Denver drafted him in the second round, 41st overall, in 2014. Nickname: the Joker.

## Passing big man

Jokić is known as a playmaking center: full-court outlets, no-look interior passes, and post vision more typical of a point guard. He often runs the offense from the elbow or the dunker spot.

## Championships and awards

He led Denver to the 2023 NBA championship, the franchise's first title, and won Finals MVP that year. He has three regular-season MVP awards (2021, 2022, and 2024).

## Draft story

Because he was a second-round pick, Jokić is often cited as one of the biggest draft steals of his era. He is listed around 6-foot-11 and is a career double-double threat in points and rebounds, with assists that rival many starting guards.
