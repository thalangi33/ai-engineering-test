# Giannis Antetokounmpo

Giannis Sina Ougko Antetokounmpo is a power forward for the Miami Heat. He was born on December 6, 1994, in Athens, Greece, to Nigerian parents, and is listed at 6-foot-11 and 243 pounds. Nickname: the Greek Freak. Milwaukee drafted him 15th overall in 2013. He is widely regarded as one of the greatest power forwards ever.

## Early life and Filathlitikos

His parents emigrated from Lagos, leaving oldest son Francis with grandparents. Giannis grew up in the Athens neighborhood of Sepolia. Without work permits, the family struggled; Giannis and brother Thanasis sold watches and handbags on the street. He started basketball in 2007. Born in Greece, he did not automatically get citizenship under jus sanguinis and was effectively stateless until Greece issued papers on May 9, 2013, weeks before the draft. He later also received a Nigerian passport.

He played for Filathlitikos, including the Greek second division in 2012–13 (9.5 points, 5.0 rebounds). He signed with CAI Zaragoza in Spain with NBA buyouts, then entered the draft. He chose jersey 34 from his parents' birth years (1963 and 1964).

## Milwaukee Bucks (2013–2026)

As a skinny 18-year-old rookie he averaged 6.8 points and made All-Rookie Second Team. By 2016–17 he led Milwaukee in points, rebounds, assists, steals, and blocks — the first player to finish a season top-20 in all five league totals — and won Most Improved Player. He became the first Greek NBA All-Star.

Under coach Mike Budenholzer the Bucks took off. Giannis won MVP in 2019 (60–22, East's best record) and again in 2020, when he also won Defensive Player of the Year, joining Michael Jordan and Hakeem Olajuwon as the only players to take both in the same season. He and LeBron James and Kareem Abdul-Jabbar are the only players with two MVPs before age 26. Toronto beat Milwaukee in the 2019 East finals; Miami beat them in the 2020 bubble.

In 2021 he signed a then-record $228 million extension. Milwaukee swept Miami, beat Kevin Durant's Nets in seven (Durant's Game 7 48), then reached the Finals even after Giannis hyperextended his knee against Atlanta. Against Phoenix he had back-to-back 40-point Finals games, blocked a Deandre Ayton alley-oop in Game 4, and closed Game 6 with 50 points, 14 rebounds, and 5 blocks. Unanimous Finals MVP. First Bucks title since 1971. He joined Jordan and Olajuwon as winners of MVP, Finals MVP, and DPOY in a career.

Later Bucks years mixed franchise records with playoff disappointment: Celtics in 2022 (first player to average 31-14-6 in a postseason), Heat in 2023 (back injury), a 64-point game without a made three in December 2023, NBA Cup MVP in 2024, a 35-15-20 game in 2025, then a first-round exit to Indiana. In 2025–26 injuries limited him to 36 games and Milwaukee missed the playoffs, snapping a nine-year streak.

## Miami Heat (2026–present)

On July 6, 2026, Milwaukee traded Antetokounmpo and Bobby Portis to Miami for Tyler Herro, Kel'el Ware, Jaime Jaquez Jr., Kasparas Jakučionis, Nate Ament's rights, and draft picks.

## Championships and honors

He has one NBA title (2021), one Finals MVP, two regular-season MVPs (2019, 2020), Defensive Player of the Year (2020), Most Improved Player (2017), All-Star Game MVP (2021, first non-American), NBA Cup title and MVP (2024), 10 All-Star selections, seven All-NBA First Teams, and four All-Defensive First Teams. He is on the NBA 75th Anniversary Team. He is Milwaukee's career leader in points, rebounds, assists, and double-doubles.

## Playing style

Size plus speed lets him cover 15 feet in one dribble and Euro-step from the arc to the rim. He is often called positionless: a point forward who can guard all five spots and roam as a free safety. Jump shooting remains a weakness (career .285 from three), which Toronto exploited with a "wall" in 2019. Playmaking is a strength; he has created more than half of Milwaukee's points while on the floor in peak seasons. Chase-down blocks are a signature.

## Greece and Olympics

He captained Greece, carried the flag at Paris 2024, and averaged a tournament-high 25.8 points before a quarterfinal loss to Germany. At EuroBasket 2025 he led Greece to bronze — the country's first medal since 2009 — and called it perhaps his greatest accomplishment as an athlete (30-17-6 in the third-place game). He scored 41 at EuroBasket 2022, the most in the event since Dirk Nowitzki in 2001.

## Off the court

Brothers Thanasis, Kostas, and Alex also played professionally; Thanasis was a Bucks teammate. The film Rise (2022) tells the family story. He is a minority owner of the Milwaukee Brewers and MLS side Nashville SC, and supports Arsenal.

Playoffs: 27.0 points, 12.2 rebounds, 5.3 assists in 84 games. Greek B / A2 with Filathlitikos (2012–13): 9.5 points and 5.0 rebounds.

Season highs include 31.1 points (2022–23), 13.6 rebounds (2019–20), 6.5 assists (2023–24 and 2024–25), and 64 points in a game. Playoff high: 33.0 points and 15.4 rebounds in 2025.
