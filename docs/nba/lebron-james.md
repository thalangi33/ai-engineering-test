# LeBron James

LeBron Raymone James Sr. is a forward for the Los Angeles Lakers. He was born on December 30, 1984, in Akron, Ohio, and entered the NBA from St. Vincent–St. Mary High School as the first overall pick in 2003.

## Career stops

He has played for the Cleveland Cavaliers (2003–2010 and 2014–2018), the Miami Heat (2010–2014), and the Lakers (2018–present). Nickname: King James.

## Championships and records

James has four NBA titles: two with Miami (2012, 2013), one with Cleveland (2016), and one with the Lakers (2020). The 2016 title is the one where Cleveland came back from a 3–1 Finals deficit against Golden State.

He became the NBA's all-time leading scorer, passing Kareem Abdul-Jabbar. He is a four-time NBA Most Valuable Player.

## Playing style

James is known for size, court vision, and transitioning from a high-usage scorer to a passer who can still finish at the rim. He has made the NBA Finals with three different franchises.
