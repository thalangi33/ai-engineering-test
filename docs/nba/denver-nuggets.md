# Denver Nuggets

The Denver Nuggets are an NBA team in the Western Conference's Northwest Division. They began in 1967 as an ABA charter franchise (Kansas City group moved to Denver), first as the Larks, then the Rockets after trucking owner Bill Ringsby, then the Nuggets in 1974 — a name taken from a 1948–50 Denver NBL/NBA club, chosen because Houston already had Rockets. They joined the NBA in the 1976 ABA–NBA merger. Home: Ball Arena (shared with the NHL Avalanche). Colors: midnight blue, sunshine yellow, Flatirons red, skyline blue. Owned by Stan Kroenke's Kroenke Sports & Entertainment.

## Championships and records

One NBA title: 2023, the franchise's first, over the Miami Heat. One conference title (2023). Twelve division titles counting two in the ABA. They were the last of the four surviving ABA teams to reach an NBA Finals and the second ABA original to win an NBA title (after San Antonio). Retired numbers include 2 (Alex English), 33 (David Thompson), 40 (Byron Beck), 44 (Dan Issel), 55 (Dikembe Mutombo), and 432 (a tribute to fans / altitude).

## Eras

Spencer Haywood averaged nearly 30 and 19.5 as an ABA MVP and Rookie of the Year in 1969–70 before jumping to the NBA, a case that opened the draft to underclassmen. Larry Brown coached 65–19 (1974–75) and 60–24 (1975–76) teams with David Thompson, Dan Issel, Bobby Jones, and Marvin Webster. They lost the 1976 ABA Finals to Julius Erving's New York Nets.

In the NBA, Doug Moe's motion offense produced 100-point games in a 136-game streak and a 126.5 points-per-game league record in 1981–82. Alex English won a scoring title; Denver and Detroit combined for 370 points on December 13, 1983 (Pistons 186–184 in triple overtime). Critics called them the "Enver Nuggets" (no D for defense). They reached the 1985 West finals and lost to the Lakers.

Paul Westhead's run-and-gun tanked the record. Mutombo, drafted in 1991, anchored the 1994 eighth seed that beat top-seeded Seattle — the first 8-over-1 upset in NBA playoff history.

Carmelo Anthony's 2000s teams made the playoffs often without a Finals trip. Nikola Jokić, a 2014 second-round pick, and Jamal Murray finally broke through: 2023 West's top seed, conference finals MVP for Jokić, then a Finals win over Miami. Jokić is the lowest-drafted MVP and Finals MVP.

## Signature players and coaches

Nikola Jokić, Jamal Murray, Alex English, David Thompson, Dan Issel, Dikembe Mutombo, Carmelo Anthony, Fat Lever, coaches Larry Brown, Doug Moe, Michael Malone, David Adelman.
