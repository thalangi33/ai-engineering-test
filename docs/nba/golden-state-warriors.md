# Golden State Warriors

The Golden State Warriors are an NBA team in the Western Conference's Pacific Division, based in San Francisco. Founded in 1946 as the Philadelphia Warriors (a BAA charter member), they moved to the Bay Area in 1962 as the San Francisco Warriors and became Golden State in 1971. Home arena: Chase Center. Colors: royal blue, yellow, black. Majority owner Joe Lacob; coach Steve Kerr. Nickname: the Dubs.

## Championships and records

Seven NBA (and BAA) titles: 1947, 1956, 1975, 2015, 2017, 2018, 2022. Seven conference titles (1975, 2015–19, 2022). Twelve division titles. They rank third in championships and Finals appearances behind the Lakers and Celtics.

The 2015–16 team went 73–9, the best regular-season record. Combined with the playoffs they hold marks for most wins in a season and the 2017 postseason run (16–1). Wilt Chamberlain and Stephen Curry are the franchise MVP winners (three awards). Curry and Klay Thompson are often called one of the greatest backcourts.

Retired numbers include 13 (Chamberlain), 14 (Tom Meschery), 16 (Al Attles), 17 (Chris Mullin), 24 (Rick Barry). The team stopped issuing Durant's 35 after he left.

## Eras

Eddie Gottlieb's Philadelphia club, led by Joe Fulks, won the first BAA title in 1947. Paul Arizin, Tom Gola, and Neil Johnston won again in 1956. Chamberlain scored 100 points in Hershey, Pennsylvania, on March 2, 1962, then went west with the franchise. After he was traded to the 76ers in 1965, Rick Barry and Nate Thurmond rebuilt the team. Barry sat out a year in a dispute, played in the ABA, and returned. Coach Al Attles and Barry swept the Washington Bullets in 1975, still called one of the biggest Finals upsets.

The 1980s were lean (the Robert Parish / Kevin McHale pick went to Boston). Run TMC — Tim Hardaway, Mitch Richmond, Chris Mullin — made the turn of the 1990s fun but not a champion.

The modern dynasty: Curry (seventh overall, 2009), Thompson, Draymond Green, and Kerr (hired 2014). First title since 1975 in 2015 (Curry's first MVP). In 2016 they were unanimous-MVP Curry's 73-win team, then blew a 3–1 Finals lead to LeBron James's Cavaliers. Kevin Durant signed in 2016; titles followed in 2017 and 2018, both Finals MVP for Durant, both against Cleveland. Toronto beat them in 2019; Durant tore his Achilles in Game 5. After two missed postseasons, Golden State beat Boston in 2022 with Curry as Finals MVP.

## Signature players and coaches

Stephen Curry, Klay Thompson, Draymond Green, Kevin Durant, Wilt Chamberlain, Rick Barry, Nate Thurmond, Chris Mullin, Tim Hardaway, coach Steve Kerr, coach Al Attles.
