# Chicago Bulls

The Chicago Bulls are an NBA team in the Eastern Conference's Central Division. Founded January 16, 1966, they play at the United Center in Chicago (shared with the NHL Blackhawks). Colors are red, black, and white. Owner Jerry Reinsdorf bought a controlling stake in 1985. They are the only NBA team with multiple titles that has never lost a Finals series.

## Championships and records

The Bulls have six NBA championships, all in the 1990s: 1991, 1992, 1993, 1996, 1997, and 1998. Those are also their six conference titles. They have nine division titles (1975, 1991–93, 1996–98, 2011, 2012). Retired numbers include 4 (Jerry Sloan), 10 (Bob Love), 23 (Michael Jordan), and 33 (Scottie Pippen).

The 1995–96 team went 72–10, the first 70-win season in NBA history. Golden State broke the win record at 73–9 in 2015–16. Jordan and Derrick Rose are the franchise's MVP winners (six awards total).

## Eras

The Bulls were the third NBA team in Chicago after the Stags and Packers/Zephyrs. Coach Johnny "Red" Kerr's 1966–67 expansion club went 33–48 and made the playoffs, then unique for an expansion team. Guy Rodgers led the league in assists. Dick Motta later coached 57-win teams built around Sloan, Bob Love, Chet Walker, and Norm Van Lier.

Artis Gilmore arrived from the ABA in 1976. In 1979 Chicago lost a coin flip for the first draft pick; Portland's Sam Bowie / Lakers' Magic Johnson story is often told, but it was Houston that won the flip for Hakeem Olajuwon while Chicago took David Greenwood second. Reinsdorf and GM Jerry Krause rebuilt around Jordan, drafted third in 1984 after Olajuwon and Bowie.

Phil Jackson replaced Doug Collins in 1989 and installed Tex Winter's triangle offense. After three Eastern Conference finals losses to Detroit's Bad Boys, Chicago swept the Pistons in 1991 and beat Magic Johnson's Lakers for the first title. Two more followed against Portland and Phoenix. Jordan retired in 1993; he returned in 1995. With Dennis Rodman, the Bulls three-peated again (1996–98), including The Flu Game and the 1998 jumper over Bryon Russell.

Since 1998 Chicago has not returned to the Finals. Rose won MVP in 2011 before an ACL tear. Rivalries include Detroit, Cleveland, and the New York Knicks.

## Signature players and coaches

Michael Jordan, Scottie Pippen, coach Phil Jackson, Dennis Rodman, Horace Grant, John Paxson, Toni Kukoč, Derrick Rose, Joakim Noah. Benny the Bull is the mascot, introduced in the Pat Williams era.
