# Milwaukee Bucks

The Milwaukee Bucks are an NBA team in the Eastern Conference's Central Division. Founded January 22, 1968, they play at Fiserv Forum. A fan contest picked the name (white-tailed deer, Wisconsin's state animal) over Robins. Colors include Good Land green and Cream City cream. Wes Edens, Jimmy Haslam, and partners bought the club from longtime owner Herb Kohl in 2014. They are the only NBA team with a championship from both the Eastern and Western Conferences.

## Championships and records

Two NBA titles: 1971 and 2021. Three conference titles (West 1971 and 1974, East 2021). Nineteen division titles, including a 2019–24 run. They won the 2024 NBA Cup. Kareem Abdul-Jabbar and Giannis Antetokounmpo are the franchise MVPs (five awards) and the only Finals MVPs. Retired numbers include 1 (Oscar Robertson), 4 (Sidney Moncrief), 14 (Jon McGlocklin), 16 (Bob Lanier), 32 (Brian Winters), 33 (Kareem).

The 1970–71 team went 66–16, still a franchise record, with a then-NBA-record 20-game win streak. Winning a title in year three is among the fastest for a true expansion team in North American sports.

## Eras

The Bucks won a coin flip with Phoenix for the 1969 first pick and signed Lew Alcindor over the ABA's New York Nets. Rookie of the Year, then a trade for Oscar Robertson, then a Finals sweep of Baltimore in 1971. Alcindor publicly became Kareem Abdul-Jabbar in 1972. They lost the 1974 Finals to Boston in seven after Kareem's skyhook in Game 6 overtime. In 1975 he demanded a bigger market; Milwaukee sent him to the Lakers for Elmore Smith, Junior Bridgeman, Brian Winters, and David Meyers.

Don Nelson coached a 1980s Central power with Moncrief, Marques Johnson, Terry Cummings, and Bob Lanier: six straight division titles, but Boston or Philadelphia blocked the Finals. They swept the Celtics in a 1983 best-of-seven, a first.

The 1990s were losing seasons. Milwaukee drafted Glenn Robinson first in 1994, acquired Ray Allen, and infamously drafted Dirk Nowitzki then traded him to Dallas for Robert Traylor. George Karl's "Light It Up" Big Three (Allen, Sam Cassell, Robinson) reached the 2001 East finals and lost to Philadelphia in seven.

Giannis, picked 15th in 2013, transformed the franchise. After a 15–67 year, Mike Budenholzer's teams won 60 in 2018–19 (Giannis MVP) and another MVP plus DPOY in 2020. Toronto and Miami ended those runs. In 2021, with Jrue Holiday, they swept Miami, beat Durant's Nets in seven, and came back from 0–2 in the Finals against Phoenix. Giannis scored 50 in Game 6. Later years: Damian Lillard trade (2023), 2024 NBA Cup, then injuries and a 2025–26 miss. Giannis was traded to Miami in July 2026.
