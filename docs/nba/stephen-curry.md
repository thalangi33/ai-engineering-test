# Stephen Curry

Wardell Stephen Curry II is a point guard for the Golden State Warriors. He was born on March 14, 1988, in Akron, Ohio, and played college basketball at Davidson before the Warriors took him seventh overall in 2009.

## Three-point shooting

Curry changed NBA spacing by making the deep three a primary weapon. He is the league's all-time leader in three-pointers made. Nicknames include Chef Curry and the Baby-Faced Assassin.

## Championships and awards

He has four NBA titles with Golden State: 2015, 2017, 2018, and 2022. He won NBA MVP in 2015 and was the first unanimous MVP in 2016. He was NBA Finals MVP in 2022.

## Off-ball movement

Curry's gravity comes from constant movement, screens, and pull-up threes. Defenses must chase him around the arc, which opens layups and dump-offs for teammates like Draymond Green and Klay Thompson.
